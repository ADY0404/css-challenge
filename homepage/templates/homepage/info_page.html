1. Our Community Standards

The Computer Science Society (CSS) exists to provide a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all members, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, or technical skill level.

We expect everyone who participates in society activities—including online channels, hackathons, workshops, and study groups—to abide by this code of conduct.

2. Expected Behavior

  • Be welcoming and patient: Remember that everyone was a novice once. Answer beginner questions with clarity and respect.
  • Communicate constructively: Phrase disagreements objectively. Focus on technical trade-offs rather than subjective dismissals.
  • Practice ethical computing: Respect user privacy, follow security testing protocols, and never deploy malicious software.
  • Maintain academic honesty: You may seek guidance and references, but submit original code for challenges and project reviews.

3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited across all CSS physical and virtual spaces:

  • Harassment, discriminatory jokes, hate speech, or offensive comments.
  • Publishing or threatening to publish private information (doxxing).
  • Unsolicited commercial advertisements, spam, or scam distributions.
  • Plagiarism or submitting work that is not your own without attribution.
  • Sustained disruption of talks, workshops, or study sessions.

4. Reporting & Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please notify a CSS Executive immediately. All reports will be handled with strict confidentiality.

Violations may result in a formal warning, temporary suspension from society channels, or permanent removal from all CSS activities upon executive review.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us, including:

  • Account Profile: Name, student email address, academic year, username, and optional profile photograph.

The information collected is used exclusively for legitimate student society purposes:

Our Privacy Commitment: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or commercial third parties under any circumstances.

We implement industry-standard security safeguards including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), salted password hashing (PBKDF2/Argon2), and restricted database access to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

You have full control over your personal data. You can review and update your profile information at any time via Profile Settings. You may also request the complete deletion of your account and associated submission history by contacting privacy@css.community.

We use strictly necessary session cookies to authenticate your login and remember your UI preferences. We do not use third-party behavioral tracking or ad network cookies.
